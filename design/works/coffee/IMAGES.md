# 사흘 커피 로스터스 — images

All photographs are generated with `gemini-3-pro-image` through the shared `gen_image.py` (2K), then
cropped, levelled and exported by `scripts/prep_images.py`. Objects only: no people, faces or hands
(BRIEF §000). Each one was checked at full size for lettering, logos and warped parts: the roaster
panel, jars, bags and tags are blank; the shopfront shelf labels were softened because at full size
they carried scribble-like marks. 13 generations, none discarded.

Look: crisp digital daylight still life, true colour, no film grain, so the photographs read like a
shop that photographs its own beans. Photos that sit straight on the white page (grind piles, bean
heaps, cascara) have their paper levelled to #FFFFFF so they have no edge. The coffee colour lives
only in the photographs; the page itself is cherry, white and black.
