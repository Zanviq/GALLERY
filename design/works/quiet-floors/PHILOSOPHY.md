# Measured Slab

A movement that draws the thing between people instead of the people. Its subject is always the
layer: the plate of material that two lives share without seeing each other. Everything is shown in
section, cut clean through, so the viewer stands inside the wall and looks both ways at once.

Form is the vocabulary of the construction drawing, handled with painstaking attention. A cut line is
heavy and certain; a line seen beyond the cut is thin and quiet; the material between two cut lines is
filled with the stipple and broken triangles of aggregate, every dot placed so the texture reads as
stone and never as noise. Rooms are left almost empty. A table, a figure, a door: just enough to say
that someone lives here. The composition is stacked, never scattered; repetition of identical bays is
the rhythm, and one disturbed bay is the event.

Colour is material and paint, nothing else. Raw concrete grey for what the building is made of; two
flat, sun-faded exterior paints, a pale green and a pale sky, for what the building shows the street.
All three sit at the same lightness, so they meet without contrast and the ink line does the work.
A single saturated red is reserved for the one instrument that measures: it appears on the dial and its
digits and nowhere else, so the eye finds it instantly and trusts it.

Motion, when there is motion, is a deformation of the layer. The slab bends a few pixels, the bend runs
outward and fades; the ceiling beneath answers with arcs. It is exaggerated like a diagram, never like
an explosion. Stillness is the default state and the goal state: the finished image is the one where
the line lies straight again.

Text is lettering on the drawing: a unit number, a level, a short label set where an architect would
set it. Large words appear only when they are the sound itself. The whole must look meticulously
crafted, the product of deep expertise in both drawing and typesetting, every line weight deliberate,
every gap measured, as if the sheet were going to the printer tomorrow and would be framed after.
